why the saving applies to nearly every call

Every mainstream AI harness — Claude Code, Grok Bot, Cursor, every chat client — resends the entire conversation history on every single turn. "Short standalone requests" barely exist in real usage: turn 30 of an ordinary chat re-uploads 30 turns of context, and you pay for all of it, every time. openzoo binds the history once; each turn ships only the new message. Measured live on plain Grok Bot chat turns: $0.0118 vs $0.2226 direct per call — 94.7% saved (~18.9×). the receipts. Check it yourself: watch your own AI client's network tab re-upload the whole conversation each turn — that's the cost this removes.

honest risks

  • early, single-maintainer infrastructure — not a company with a support team
  • deposit addresses are custodial-style: software this project controls signs for them
  • TOKEN and LEOS are volatile small-cap tokens; USDC is the clean comparison
  • payments settle before the model call and are final — no refunds if an upstream errors
